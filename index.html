Engineering Services

We've led the design on major infrastructure projects — so when we produce your deliverables, we understand what matters for construction, consent, and coordination.

CAD

CAD Drafting & Documentation

Get construction-ready plans, sections, and detail sheets that pass review first time. Our engineering background means we catch coordination issues before they reach the site — not just draft what we're given.

PLN

Network Plans

Clear, fully annotated utility network plans your contractors can build from with confidence. We understand pipe networks from a design perspective, so alignments, nodes, and connections are documented with construction logic — not just geometry.

PRF

Profiles for Gravity Systems

Gravity system profiles that account for real-world constraints — inverts, gradients, and hydraulic grade lines checked against design intent. Because we've designed these systems ourselves, we know what to look for before it becomes an RFI on site.

RSM

Rising Main Profiles

Pressurised main documentation with pump connections, air valves, and scour points correctly located and annotated. Our three waters design experience means your rising main profiles reflect engineering judgement, not just data entry.

3D

Civil 3D Modelling

Surfaces, corridors, and pipe networks modelled with engineering understanding — not just software skills. You get accurate profiles, sections, and quantity take-offs that reflect design intent and are ready for construction issue.

BIM

BIM Federation & Clash Detection

Reduce rework and coordination surprises with disciplined BIM federation. We've coordinated up to eight disciplines on a single project — we know how to structure models, track clashes, and drive resolution so your project stays on programme.

VIZ

InfraWorks Visualisations

Win stakeholder buy-in with clear, compelling visual models of your project. Our visualisations are built on real engineering data, so what stakeholders see is what gets built — bridging the gap between technical design and public understanding.

GEO

Earthworks & Grading Models

Optimise your earthworks costs with accurate cut/fill models built by someone who understands platform design. We balance volumes with practical construction staging — so your quantities are reliable from tender through to completion.

ROW

Road Geometry & Utility Layouts

Road alignments and utility layouts modelled with traffic, safety, and constructability in mind. Our road design experience across state highways and local roads means your geometry works in the real world, not just on screen.

WTR

Three Waters Pipe Networks

Water supply, wastewater, and stormwater networks modelled and documented to NZ standards. Having led three waters design on projects like the Kaitaia trunk main, we bring pipeline engineering knowledge to every network model we produce.

Designed approach road geometry for the Waitohi ferry terminal redevelopment — accommodating forecast traffic volumes, oversized loads, and abnormal vehicles for Interislander's new fleet.

Design lead and BIM coordinator — integrating eight design disciplines into a single federated model for the new Nelson transit hub.

Disaster Recovery

Designed reinstatement works and earthworks rehabilitation across storm-damaged road networks in the Marlborough Sounds, restoring access to isolated communities.

Outcome: Successfully accelerated the repair of critical roading assets by rapidly modeling complex geotechnical hazards, restoring community access months ahead of schedule.

Lead designer for a 12 km, 500 mm diameter trunk main connecting source to treatment plant — a major water supply upgrade for the Far North.
